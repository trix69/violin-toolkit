## Notes

- **Licences.** Public Domain and CC0 need nothing. CC BY 4.0 (Canon) and CC BY-SA 3.0 (Gavotte, Air,
  Spring, Winter) require credit to the edition, which the app's Score credits window gives (title,
  composer, edition, typesetter, licence). The share-alike editions are shipped as our MusicXML conversions of their LilyPond sources
  (and Winter with its tempo changed), noted in each row and in the app's credits; the converted files stay
  under CC BY-SA 3.0. Ave Maria (OpenScore, CC0) needs nothing; its credit is given anyway.
- **Our own work.** Twinkle (engraved here) and the violin lines drawn out of Ode to Joy and the
  Mountain King are ours; they may be used under CC0 1.0.
- **File names.** The phone apps (1.38 and later) download built-in tunes from the website by file name,
  so existing names are kept even where the content is now MIDI (`ode.mxl` and `mountain_king.mxl`
  hold MIDI; the player reads files by content). The `.mid` files of the
  older names (`twinkle`, `ode`, `mountain_king`, `canon`, `winter`, and `night_music.mid` for the Serenade) carry
  the same tunes as MIDI for the 1.37 apps, which read MIDI only.
- **What was removed.** The previous six files (arrangements by third parties with no verifiable
  licence: Toninelli, López, Atimony12, an unnamed uploader, a dead MuseScore.com link) are gone from
  `src/midi/`; none of those names remains in the shipped files or the built page.
- **Formats.** Mutopia publishes LilyPond, MIDI and PDF, not MusicXML. Seven of its editions (Minuet,
  Gavotte, Air, Canon, Spring, Winter, Serenade) are shipped as MusicXML converted from their LilyPond sources,
  checked note for note against the edition's own MIDI (same notes and timing; the MIDI writes out trills and
  tremolos that the score shows as signs). Ode and the Mountain King stay MIDI (our violin lines). Twinkle and
  Ave Maria are MusicXML.
