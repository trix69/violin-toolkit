Violin Toolkit Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-15

Violin Toolkit is a free violin practice app (tuner, fingerboard, metronome, MIDI player, fingerings, Play-along and music symbols). It has no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and it never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. The only thing it counts is the version check described below.

Microphone

The tuner, Pitch Check and Play-along use the microphone to hear what you play. The sound is analysed on your device, as it happens, and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Play-along listens only during a take, from Start until it stops, and compares each note with the score; it keeps just its per-note results (pitch and timing), only while the app is open, and they are gone when you close it or choose another tune. The microphone is only used after you allow it, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (language, appearance, tuning pitch and similar), your practice time per day, the built-in tunes you have downloaded, and any MIDI or MusicXML files you import are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Internet

The app works offline, except that a built-in tune not inside the app needs one download first. Only Twinkle and Ode come inside the app. Every version (the website, and the Android and iPhone apps) goes online only to download a built-in tune the first time you play it, after which it is kept on the device; in the Android and iPhone apps, to check, before playing a built-in tune already saved on the device, whether the website has a newer version of it; and to check whether a newer version of the app is available. All of these come from the app's own website, trix69.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages. No personal information is sent; as with any website, GitHub can see your device's IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). The app opens your web browser only when you tap a link that leads outside it, and it asks you first. In every version this includes Report an issue (Settings › Help), which opens GitHub's new-issue page with the app version and which kind of app it is already filled in; nothing is sent unless you submit the issue yourself, and that needs a GitHub account. The website and the Android app downloaded from it also have the project page and the support link. Once you leave the app, that website's own privacy policy applies. The update button opens the app's own store page (Google Play or the App Store) directly, without asking.

To tell you when a newer version is out, the app asks a small version service (Cloudflare) at launch. That request carries the app version, which kind of app it is (browser, installed web app, Android app, Google Play app, iPhone app) and a random identifier created on this device. We record the date and time, those three values and nothing else: no IP address, no location, no cookies, no personal details. As with any website, Cloudflare can see your device's IP address when the request arrives; we do not store it. Turning off 'Automatic update check' in Settings stops the request entirely.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/trix69/violin-toolkit.

小提琴工具箱隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-15

小提琴工具箱（Violin Toolkit）是一款免费的小提琴练习应用（调音器、指板、节拍器、MIDI 播放器、指法谱、跟音练习和乐谱符号）。没有账户、没有广告、不使用 Cookie，也不会索取你的姓名、邮箱或其他个人信息。它唯一统计的内容是下文所述的版本检查。

麦克风

调音器、音准检查和跟音练习会使用麦克风来听你的演奏。声音只在你的设备上即时分析，不会被录音、保存或发送到任何地方。跟音练习只在一次演奏期间收音（从“开始”到结束），把每个音和乐谱对比；它只保留每个音的结果（音准和节奏），而且只在应用打开时保留，关闭应用或换一首曲子后就会清除。只有在你允许后才会使用麦克风，你也可以随时在手机设置中关闭这项权限。

保存在设备上的内容

你的设置（语言、外观、标准音高等）、每天的练习时间、你下载过的内置曲目以及你导入的 MIDI 或 MusicXML 文件只保存在本应用在你设备上的存储空间中，不会离开设备。卸载应用或清除其存储数据即会删除这些内容。

网络

本应用可以离线使用，但未包含在应用中的内置曲目需要先下载一次；应用里只自带《小星星》和《欢乐颂》。所有版本（网页版以及安卓和 iPhone 应用）只在以下情况下联网：第一次播放某首内置曲目时下载该曲目，之后保存在设备上；在安卓和 iPhone 应用里，播放已保存在设备上的内置曲目之前，检查网站上是否有它的新版本；以及检查应用是否有新版本。这些都来自本应用自己的网站 trix69.github.io（由 GitHub Pages 托管）。不会发送任何个人信息；与访问任何网站一样，GitHub 能看到你设备的 IP 地址（参见 GitHub 的隐私声明）。只有在你点按通往应用外部的链接时，本应用才会打开浏览器，而且会先询问你。所有版本都有“报告问题”（设置 › 帮助），它会打开 GitHub 的新建 issue 页面，并预先填好应用版本和应用类型；只有你自己提交 issue 时才会发送，提交需要 GitHub 账户。网站及从网站下载的安卓应用另有项目主页和支持链接。离开应用后，适用该网站自己的隐私政策。更新按钮会直接打开本应用自己的商店页面（Google Play 或 App Store），不会先询问。

为了在有新版本时告诉你，应用在启动时会询问一个小型版本服务（Cloudflare）。该请求包含应用版本、应用类型（浏览器、已安装的网页应用、安卓应用、Google Play 应用、iPhone 应用）以及在这台设备上生成的一个随机标识。我们只记录日期、时间和这三项，别的都不记录：没有 IP 地址、没有位置、没有 Cookie、没有个人信息。与访问任何网站一样，请求到达时 Cloudflare 能看到你设备的 IP 地址；我们不会保存它。在设置中关闭“自动检查更新”即可完全停止这个请求。

儿童

本应用不会收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更与联系

如本政策有变更，新版本会连同新日期发布在本页面。如有疑问，请在 github.com/trix69/violin-toolkit 提交 issue。
