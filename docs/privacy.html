Violin Toolkit Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-12

Violin Toolkit is a free violin practice app (tuner, fingerboard, metronome, MIDI player and music symbols). It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Microphone

The tuner and Pitch Check use the microphone to hear the note you play. The sound is analysed on your device, as it happens, and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The microphone is only used after you allow it, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (language, appearance, tuning pitch and similar), your practice time per day, and any MIDI or MusicXML files you import are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Internet

The app works offline. The Android and iPhone apps go online only to download a built-in tune that is not already in the app, the first time you play it, and to check whether a newer version is available. Both come from the app's own website, trix69.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages. No personal information is sent; as with any website, GitHub can see your device's IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). The website and the Android app downloaded from it open your web browser only if you tap an outside link, such as the support link; that website's own privacy policy then applies. The Google Play and iPhone apps have no outside links; their only way out is the update button, which opens the app's own store page.

To tell you when a newer version is out, the app asks a small version service (Cloudflare) at launch. That request carries the app version, which kind of app it is (browser, installed web app, Android app, Google Play app, iPhone app) and a random identifier created on this device. We record the date and time, those three values and nothing else: no IP address, no location, no cookies, no personal details. Turning off 'Automatic update check' in Settings stops the request entirely.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/trix69/violin-toolkit.

Violin Toolkit 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-12

Violin Toolkit 是一款免费的小提琴练习应用（调音器、指板、节拍器、MIDI 播放器和乐谱符号）。没有账户、没有广告、没有统计分析，也不会收集、发送或分享任何个人数据。

麦克风

调音器和音准检查会使用麦克风来听你拉的音。声音只在你的设备上即时分析，不会被录音、保存或发送到任何地方。只有在你允许后才会使用麦克风，你也可以随时在手机设置中关闭这项权限。

保存在设备上的内容

你的设置（语言、外观、标准音高等）、每天的练习时间以及你导入的 MIDI 或 MusicXML 文件只保存在本应用在你设备上的存储空间中，不会离开设备。卸载应用或清除其存储数据即会删除这些内容。

网络

本应用可以离线使用。安卓和 iPhone 应用只在两种情况下联网：第一次播放某首尚未包含在应用中的内置曲目时下载该曲目，以及检查是否有新版本。两者都来自本应用自己的网站 trix69.github.io（由 GitHub Pages 托管）。不会发送任何个人信息；与访问任何网站一样，GitHub 能看到你设备的 IP 地址（参见 GitHub 的隐私声明）。网站及从网站下载的安卓应用只有在你点按外部链接（例如支持链接）时才会打开浏览器；届时适用该网站自己的隐私政策。Google Play 和 iPhone 应用中没有外部链接，唯一的例外是更新按钮，它会打开本应用自己的商店页面。

为了在有新版本时告诉你，应用在启动时会询问一个小型版本服务（Cloudflare）。该请求包含应用版本、应用类型（浏览器、已安装的网页应用、安卓应用、Google Play 应用、iPhone 应用）以及在这台设备上生成的一个随机标识。我们只记录日期、时间和这三项，别的都不记录：没有 IP 地址、没有位置、没有 Cookie、没有个人信息。在设置中关闭“自动检查更新”即可完全停止这个请求。

儿童

本应用不会收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更与联系

如本政策有变更，新版本会连同新日期发布在本页面。如有疑问，请在 github.com/trix69/violin-toolkit 提交 issue。
